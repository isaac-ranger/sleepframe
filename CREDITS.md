## Stars

`game/stars.dat` holds the positions, brightness and colours of 9,096 stars from the *Bright Star Catalogue, 5th Revised Ed.* (Hoffleit D., Warren Jr W.H., 1991), catalogue V/50 at the Centre de Données astronomiques de Strasbourg. `stars/make_stars.py` makes it from the catalogue.
